### Community theatre in Surrey and Hampshire

Brightlight Theatre Company is an award-winning community theatre group based in Frimley, serving audiences and members across Camberley, Farnborough, Ash and the surrounding area.

We produce three main shows each year alongside festival productions, new writing and workshops. Whether you're looking for live theatre, an amateur dramatics group to join, or opportunities in acting, directing, backstage work, lighting, sound or stage management, we'd love to hear from you.
