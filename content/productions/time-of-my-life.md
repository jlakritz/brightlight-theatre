# Time Of My Life

Brightlight Theatre are pleased to announce that our next production will be Alan Ayckbourn's fantastic comedy "Time Of My Life".  
  
It is Laura's 54th birthday and Gerry has organised a small family dinner at their favourite restaurant. All seems well. Their eldest son, Glyn, is now back together with his long-suffering wife Stephanie, and their younger son, Adam has brought along his new girlfriend, an outrageous hairdresser, to meet the family for the first time. But life isn't quite as simple as it would seem - as we find out over the course of the evening.  
  
We are performing this on Saturday 11th April at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 18th April at both 2pm and 7:30pm at St Peter's Church Hall in Frimley.  
  
Tickets are only £8 (£5 for over-60s and students). You can buy online through our [Box Office](http://brightlighttheatre.ticketsource.co.uk/) or by calling or texting [07546 737813](tel:07546737813). Not suitable for under-14s.
