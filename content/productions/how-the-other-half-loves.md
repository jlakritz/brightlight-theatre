# How The Other Half Loves

**Three couples. Two dinner parties. One hilarious farce.**

Meet The Fosters – Frank & Fiona. They seem perfectly normal. So do The Philips – Bob & Teresa. And of course The Featherstones – William & Mary. All perfectly respectable couples.

Or are they?

When Fiona returns home suspiciously late one night, misunderstandings, mishaps and mistaken identities lead to two disastrous dinner parties. Will you be able to keep up with all the lies?

With ingenious staging and Ayckbourn's signature wit, this hilarious comedy takes you on a journey of confusion, laughter, and unexpected revelations. Don't miss it.

**Saturday 2nd September at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 9th September at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**
