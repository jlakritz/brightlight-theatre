# Songkeeper

"Songkeepers only come when you're lost. To show you the way."

We’re thrilled to announce that Brightlight Theatre has been selected to perform at this year’s National Drama Festival in Bromsgrove!

Our original play, Songkeeper, will be taking the stage on Saturday 2nd August, representing the very best of amateur theatre. Following a sensational preview in Frimley and multiple awards at the Woking and Welwyn Drama Festivals, we can’t wait to share this powerful and visually striking piece with a national audience.

Songkeeper blends puppetry, storytelling, and theatrical invention in a haunting tale of hope, memory and light.

We’re proud to be part of this prestigious event — wish us luck!
