# The Importance of Being Earnest

**Earnest is engaged to Gwendolen.  
But he's also engaged to Cecily.  
And the other problem is he doesn't exist.  
Yikes.**

When Jack and Algernon create an alter ego called Earnest, no one can keep up with the lies. Through cucumber sandwiches, mistaken identities, proposals, christenings and handbags, things eventually catch up with them.

Brightlight are proud to present this hilarious production of Oscar Wilde's classic romantic comedy. Don't miss it, and don't forget your handbags!

**Saturday 3rd September at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 10th September at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £10 (£8 for under-16s & over-60s).

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.
