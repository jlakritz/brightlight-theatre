## Two centuries. Countless voices. One community story.

A brand new play two hundred years in the making. Written in collaboration with the local community, celebrating the bicentennial of St Peter's Church, Frimley.

**She's turning two hundred today. Her bones are woodwormed timber. Her joints are rust-set bolts. And she remembers everything.**

200 follows Rosemaria - the beating heart of a Surrey village - older than memory itself, as she's visited on her birthday by the stories she's kept safe for two centuries. A winder of music boxes who loses her purpose overnight. A wife who writes letters to a husband who may never read them. A brother and sister squabbling over party bunting while their own family history spills out of a cardboard box.

Bold, funny, and unafraid to break your heart, 200 moves between fable and village hall, between wartime letters and wind-up toys, asking the same question in every era: what do we keep, what do we let go, and what stays with us no matter how much the world changes around it?

This is Brightlight Theatre's most ambitious new work yet - an original piece, built from real history and pure imagination, performed in the very building it celebrates.
