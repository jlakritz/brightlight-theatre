## Two centuries. Countless voices. One community story.

**200 - a new play inspired by the stories of life at St. Peter’s Church. 200 years in the making.**

A brand-new play inspired by the real-life tales of St. Peter’s Church from the last two centuries. As we celebrate the building's bicentennial, we are working hand-in-hand with the local community to develop this script.

**Saturday 29th August at 7.30pm and again on Saturday 5th September at 2.00pm and 7.30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £11 (£9 for under-16s & over-60s).

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community
