# The Christmas Carbuncle

In December 2014 Brightlight Theatre presented their Christmas production - Sherlock Holmes in the Curious Case of the Christmas Carbuncle.  
Come and join Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson as they solve a mystery every bit as perplexing as it is festive. Will they find the priceless missing jewel? Will Holmes find his Christmas spirit? Will Dr. Watson ever get something to eat? Find out the answers to all these questions and more as Brightlight Theatre take you on an adventure to remember.  
We performed on Saturday 6th December at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 20th December at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Chuch Hall, Frimley.  
As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales were donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/).
