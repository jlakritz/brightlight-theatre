# A Christmas Carol

Brightlight are pleased to announce our Christmas show this year will be a fantastic and magical retelling of the Charles Dickens classic - A Christmas Carol. We will be performing at St. Peter's Church Hall in Frimley, and staging two performances on the 21st December - a matinee at 2:30pm and evening performance at 7:30pm. Tickets cost £7 for adults and £3 for children under the age of 14. Tickets are available to buy [online here](http://brightlighttheatre.ticketsource.co.uk/) or by calling our box office on 07533 146019.
