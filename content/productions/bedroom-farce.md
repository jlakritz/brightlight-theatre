# Bedroom Farce

Throw back the covers and get ready to laugh as Brightlight Theatre present this hilarious comedy by the magnificent Alan Ayckbourn.

When Trevor and Susannah visit three different bedrooms over the course of one evening, they leave a trail of choas, confusion and catastrophe in a classic Ayckbourn comedy.

Three bedrooms. Four couples. What could possibly go wrong?

**Saturday 22nd April at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 29th April at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £8 (£5 for over-60s). Not suitable for under-16s.

Book online through the Box Office or by calling or texting 07546 737813.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.
