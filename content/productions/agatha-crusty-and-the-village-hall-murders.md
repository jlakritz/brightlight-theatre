## "Well, you won't find any murders round here. Nothing much ever happens in Chortleby!"

**Famous last words...**

When crime novelist Agatha Crusty arrives in the sleepy village of Chortleby for Christmas, she expects a quiet visit. Instead, she finds herself caught up in a series of mysterious deaths connected to the village hall committee. And with an investigating officer as incompetent as DI Twigg, the laughs pile up as fast as the bodies. 

As the Christmas panto rehearsals continue, tensions rise, secrets emerge and the body count climbs. Can Agatha work out who's behind it all before anyone else meets an untimely end?

A festive comedy murder-mystery to kick off your Christmas, from Frimley's award-winning Brightlight Theatre.
