# Don't Dress For Dinner

Get ready to laugh with Brightlight Theatre as they present the hilarious farce, Don't Dress For Dinner. With his wife out of town, an alibi from his best friend, and a gourmet chef to add culinary spice, Bernard has the perfect romantic evening planned with his mistress — or so he thinks.

**Saturday 9th April at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 16th April at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are only £8 (£5 for over-60s). Book online through the [Box Office](https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/brightlighttheatre) or by calling or texting 07546 737813. Suitable for ages 16 and over.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.

We really hope to see you there!

This amateur production of “Don't Dress for Dinner” is presented by special arrangement with SAMUEL FRENCH, LTD.
