# The Start of Something

Brightlight Theatre are proud to present their first ever entry into Woking Drama Festival

When Amy finds a letter, lost and forgotten under her floorboards, she sets in motion a chain of events that will change the lives of three strangers forever.

Told through three separate stories of women trying to come to terms with love, loss and loneliness, we hear from Amy, struggling to cope with the loss of her husband, Emma, struggling to keep her marriage together, and Evelyn, struggling to find love for the first time.

The lives of the three women slowly intertwine, and we learn that even endings bring with them the start of something.

The Start of Something will be presented on Friday 14th October at 7:30pm at the Rhoda McGaw Theatre in Woking, and stars Lynn Price, Thea Reeves and Denise Butcher. More details and tickets are available at [www.wokingdramafestival.co.uk](http://www.wokingdramafestival.co.uk)
