# Blithe Spirit

“Do you mean to tell me that having conjured up this ghost or spirit or whatever she is, you are unable to do anything about it at all?”

Charles Condomine thinks it'll be fun to host a dinner party with a séance. But when madcap medium Madame Arcati accidentally conjures up the ghost of Charles' ex-wife, Elvira, things get a little bit complicated, to say the least.

Elvira plans to have Charles to herself forever, but his new wife, Ruth, might have something to say about that - if only she could see or hear the ghost. As the haunting hilarity escalates, the only option is to call Madame Arcati back to fix things... but will she just make them worse?

Noël Coward's classic comedy, full of sparkling wit & glorious belly laughs. A supernatural sensation that you won't want to miss!
