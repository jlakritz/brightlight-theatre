# The Edge of Darkness

**"Three years ago the daughter - Emma - disappeared. Off the face of the earth. Disappeared without trace.**  
**But they found her again.  
And they're bringing her back today."**

After her disappearance several years ago, Emma is finally found. But, suffering from severe amnesia, she barely remembers her parents let alone what happened to her. As the family attempt to return to a normal life many unanswered questions surface. Who exactly is Emma?

A psychological thriller sure to keep you on the _edge_ of your seat.

**Saturday 23rd April at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 30th April at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £10 (£8 for under-16s & over-60s).

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.
