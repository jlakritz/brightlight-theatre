## Since I began parish life, I've boiled 7,000 gallons of water and poured 200,000 cups of tea. I've also baked 4,600 medium sized quiches and personally made 2 tons of shortcrust pastry

**Entertaining Angels by Richard Everett.**

Grace has spent a lifetime being on her best behaviour. As the dutiful wife of a vicar, she has poured enough tea to float a battleship and swallowed enough biting comments to choke a horse. Now that her husband, Bardolph, has passed away, Grace is ready to hang up her "vicar’s wife" halo and enjoy a quiet life of freedom in her beloved garden. There is just one problem: the vicarage - and the vicar - aren't quite done with her yet.

Richard Everett’s *Entertaining Angels* is a sharp-edged comedy with a deeply beating heart. Filled with dry British wit, tea, turmoil, and the secrets of a marriage, this is a touching, funny story about asking for forgiveness and the messy business of being a family.
