# Almost Holmes

The Game's (Almost) Afoot!

We are now SOLD OUT. You can still join the waitlist to be notified if tickets become available.  

---

**One bet. One missing musician. One landlady pretending to be Sherlock Holmes!**

It’s Christmas 1893. When Watson wagers that women can’t solve crimes, Mrs. Hudson dons the deerstalker to prove him wrong. But what begins as a playful challenge soon spirals into vanishing violinists, eccentric suspects, secret passageways, stolen jewels, and a galloping chase across Victorian London.

To win the bet - and her dignity - Mrs. Hudson must outwit villains, rescue a damsel, and prove that deduction is no man’s game.

Packed with laughs, wit, and a sprinkle of Christmas cheer, this festive caper is perfect for adults and families alike - clever enough for grown-ups, cheeky enough to keep the kids giggling (even if they don’t quite get all the jokes!).
