# Welcome To Our Village, Please Invade Carefully

**_Now, you should all have a copy of the agenda – between item nine, repairs to the fence between the playground and Colney Drive, and item ten, any other business, could you please add item nine b, the recent invasion of the village by beings from another world._**

When aliens invade the quaint village of Cresdon Green the residents are unsure how to respond. Should they submit to their new overlords, or should they form a resistance and save the world from further invasion. And more to the point, will the invaders ever get the hang of cricket?

The hit comedy as heard on BBC Radio 4 comes to the stage.

**Saturday 1st September at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 8th September at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £8 (£5 for under-16s & over-60s).

Book online through the Box Office or by calling or texting 07546 737813.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.

This amateur production of “Welcome To Our Village, Please Invade Carefully” is presented by special arrangement with Samuel French Ltd.
