# The 5-Year Charity Spectacular

**_All your favourite scenes from (almost) every play we've performed!_**

For one night only, Brightlight Theatre are proud to celebrate their fifth year in Frimley. Come and see extracts from almost every play we've ever performed including The 39 Steps, Don't Dress For Dinner, Bedroom Farce and our award-winning production of The Start of Something.

All profits will be donated to local cancer charity [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) to support the amazing work they do for cancer patients in our community.

**Saturday 7th July at 7:30pm for **one night only** at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £8 (£5 over-60s). Some scenes contain swearing and may not be suitable for young children.

Book online through the Box Office or by calling or texting 07546 737813.
