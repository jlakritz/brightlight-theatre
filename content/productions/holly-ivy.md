# Holly & Ivy

4 Christmas stories, 8 connected characters, 1 butternut squash. It's a bit like a stage version of Love Actually, but with more vegan fudge!

Follow the stories of eight residents of Holly Road and the Ivy Farm Estate as they prepare for Christmas. Can Lorna and Keith keep their am dram production of The Nativity on the rails? Will Hillary or Anabel win Best Stall at the Christmas market? How will Alex react when he catches a burglar in his house? And will Holly and Will ever make it to the big family Christmas dinner?

Join Brightlight Theatre this Christmas as they perform four brand new christmas comedies from local award-winning playwright, Jamie Lakritz. It's sure to start your festive season with a bang.
