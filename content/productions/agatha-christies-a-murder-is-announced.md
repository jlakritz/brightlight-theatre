# Agatha Christie's A Murder Is Announced

**A murder is announced and will take place on Friday, December the eighteenth, at Little Paddocks - at six thirty p.m.**

When an advert in the local paper states the time and place a murder is due to occur at Miss Blacklock's house in the small, sleepy village of Chipping Cleghorn, the residents are astonished. Could it be a funny practical joke? Or maybe a malicious threat? Or worst of all - could it be the work of a cold-blooded killer?

The group gathers at the appointed time. Suddenly the lights go out and a shot is fired. Luckily, Miss Marple is on hand to unmask the identity of the murderer in Agatha Christie's classic mystery...
