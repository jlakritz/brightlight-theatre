# I'll Be Home For Christmas

Bring in the festive season with Brightlight Theatre as they present the world premiere of their original heart-warming comedy, I'll Be Home For Christmas. Moving backwards and forwards from wartime Britain to present day, follow the adventures of a range of colourful characters as they struggle to get home for Christmas.

Florence dreams of spending Christmas with her family, but with her husband fighting in France and her son evacuated to the countryside, will her dream come true? Will university student Kate get home in time for Christmas dinner? Will Field Marshall Montgomery, the cat, ever be found? The answers to all these questions and more are sure to set your Christmas spirits soaring.

**Saturday 5th December at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 12th December at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are only £8 (£5 for over-60s or under-16s). Book online through the [Box Office](https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/brightlighttheatre) or by calling or texting 07546 737813. Suitable for all the family.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.

We really hope to see you there!
