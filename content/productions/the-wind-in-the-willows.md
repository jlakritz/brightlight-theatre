# The Wind In The Willows

Brightlight Theatre are proud to announce a new production of Kenneth Grahame's classic, The Wind In The Willows, adapted by Jamie Lakritz. Telling the adventures of the animals of the Riverbank and Wild Wood, follow Toad's adventures in cars, in prisons and in every sort of trouble!

This production is a one-off charity event - Brightlight will donate **all** profits from this production to [The Zone](http://www.the-zone.org.uk/), a local charity for families living with autism and similar conditions in Hart, Rushmoor and surrounding areas.

**Saturday 28th May at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 4th June at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Please note that the 2pm performance on 4th June will be a relaxed performance.

Tickets are only £8 (£5 for over-60s). Book online through the [Box Office](https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/brightlighttheatre) or by calling or texting 07546 737813. Suitable for all ages.

We really hope to see you there!
