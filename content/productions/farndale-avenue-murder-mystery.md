# Farndale Avenue Murder Mystery

It's not just the play that goes wrong... it's everything!

The Farndale Avenue Housing Estate Townswomen's Guild Dramatic Society Murder Mystery! by David McGillivray and Walter Zerlin Jnr.

When the enthusiastic members of Farndale's Dramatic Society attempt a murder mystery, everything that can go wrong, does - with more mishaps, mayhem, and missed lines than you can shake a magnifying glass at!

---

**Saturday 6th September at 7.30pm and again on Saturday 13th September at 2.00pm and 7.30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £10 (£8 for under-16s & over-60s).

  

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.
