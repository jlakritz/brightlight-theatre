# Murdered To Death

Get ready to laugh with Brightlight Theatre as they present the hilarious murder mystery, Murdered To Death by Peter Gordon.

The inept and bungling Inspector Pratt battles against the odds and his own incompetence to solve a murder at Bagshot House. It soon becomes clear that the murderer isn’t finished yet; will the culprit be unmasked before everyone else has met their doom or will the audience die laughing first?

**Saturday 3rd Sept at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 10th Sept at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £8 (£5 for over-60s).

Book online through the Box Office or by calling or texting 07546 737813.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.

We really hope to see you there!
