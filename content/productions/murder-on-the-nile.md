# Murder On The Nile

Get ready to join Brightlight Theatre for a murderous trip on the Nile as they present the classic Agatha Christie whodunnit. Fatal circumstances await a newlywed couple as they embark on their honeymoon voyage down the famous Egyptian river. Clues and red herrings aplenty, the only sure way to get out alive is to ... "Suspect Everyone!"

**Saturday 12th September at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 19th September at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are only £8 (£5 for over-60s or under-16s). Book online through the [Box Office](https://www.ticketsource.co.uk/brightlighttheatre) or by calling or texting 07546 737813. Suitable for all ages, though expect a few loud gunshots and themes of murder.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.

We really hope to see you there!

This amateur production of "Murder On The Nile" is presented by special arrangement with Samuel French Ltd.
