# It's A Wonderful Life - A Live Radio Play

'It's a Wonderful Life' is based on the story, 'The Greatest Gift' by Philip Van Doren Stern  

**"Remember, no man is a failure who has friends."**

The beloved American holiday classic comes to captivating life as a live 1940s radio broadcast complete with live sound effects. With the help of an ensemble that brings a few dozen characters to the stage, the story of idealistic George Bailey unfolds as he contemplates his life one fateful Christmas Eve.

Sure to get you into the Christmas spirit.

**Saturday 25th November at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 3rd December at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £10 (£8 for under-16s & over-60s).

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.
