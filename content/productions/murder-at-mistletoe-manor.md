# Murder! At Mistletoe Manor

Who-ho-ho-ho-dunnit?!

Christmas Eve, 1927. A colourful collection of passengers, (or should that be suspects?), assemble in a train station waiting room. Little do they know there is a murderer in their midst. When tragedy strikes everybody has a motive, everybody has an alibi... and nobody has a clue.

The rich aristocrat. The Scottish historian.

The Russian countess. The American tourist.

The maid. The novelist. The actress.

The list goes on.

With five actors playing twelve characters, complete with quick changes and questionable accents, will world-famous detective Montgomery Sterling catch the killer? Or will the audience laugh themselves to death first?

**Can you solve the Murder! At Mistletoe Manor?**
