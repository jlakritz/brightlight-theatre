# The 39 Steps

By Arrangement with Edward Snape for Fiery Angel Limited  
John Buchan and Alfred Hitchcock’s  
THE 39 STEPS  
Adapted by Patrick Barlow  
From an original concept by Simon Corble and Nobby Dimon.

**The hilarious west-end smash hit, The 39 Steps, comes to Frimley in September courtesy of Brightlight Theatre.**

Our dashing hero, Richard Hannay, finds himself part of a spy conspiracy when a mysterious woman dies in his apartment. Will he survive the trains, planes, automobiles, bridges, bagpipes, bullets, highlands, hotels and handcuffs that he encounters on his adventure to discover what the 39 Steps really are? And will the cast of 8 playing over 150 different roles survive three performances? There's only one way to find out.

Featuring all your favourite scenes from the John Buchan novel and Alfred Hitchcock film (plus a few more on the side!)

**Saturday 16th September at 7:30pm and again on Saturday 23rd September at both 2:00pm and 7:30pm at St. Peter's Church Hall, Frimley, GU16 7AQ.**

Tickets are £8 (£5 for under-16s & over-60s).

Book online through the Box Office or by calling or texting 07546 737813.

As with all Brightlight productions, at least 10% of ticket sales will be donated to local cancer charity, [KatCanDo](http://www.katcando.org.uk/) in support of the amazing work they do for our community.

This amateur production of “39 Steps, The” is presented by special arrangement with SAMUEL FRENCH, LTD.
