St Peter's Church in Frimley turns 200 this year - and we want to mark it with something nobody else could write.

We're creating a brand new play woven from real memories and stories from across the generations, and we need your help to do it. This is the venue Brightlight calls home, and now we want it to belong to the whole community on stage too.

Have you attended services there? Celebrated a milestone? Got a memory - *funny, moving, ordinary or extraordinary* - that's tied to this building? That's exactly what we're after.

**What does the church mean to you? What moments do you remember?** Your stories will help shape this new work from the ground up.

Get in touch at [200@brightlight-theatre.co.uk](mailto:200@brightlight-theatre.co.uk) — we'd love to hear from you.
