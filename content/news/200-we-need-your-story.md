To celebrate the bicentennial of St Peter's Church in Frimley (the venue we perform at), we're hoping to create a new play that weaves together stories and memories from across the generations. But we need your help!

We really want the local community to be involved in creating this piece of theatre. To start with, we'd love to hear your memories of St Peter's - whether you've attended services, celebrated milestones there, or simply have a connection to this special place that's been part of our community for 200 years.

What does the church mean to you? What moments do you remember? Your stories will help shape this new work.

Please get in touch at 200@brightlight-theatre.co.uk – we'd be delighted to hear from you.
